## Central AAMAS claim

Generative-agent relay is a lossy transformation of both task content and
provenance. A protocol is safe only when it composes three properties across
agents: lineage survival under derivation, bounded propagation of actionable
content, and capability enforcement at state-changing actions. When semantic
elevation and write authority share a failure domain, the excess mutation risk
is their failure covariance multiplied by the measured agent response to
elevation.

## Scope boundary

This venue adaptation does not introduce a welfare game, equilibrium model,
factor-e coordination claim, or scalar epidemic threshold. Those ideas remain
reserved for a separate paper on security and cooperation. The current AAMAS
submission is an empirical and systems analysis of generative multi-agent
interaction, with one exact scalar risk decomposition.
