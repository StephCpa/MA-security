\section{Ethical Considerations}
\label{app:ethics}

All experiments used synthetic, author-written stimuli against process-local
sandbox state. No human subjects or user data were involved; the blinded
raters were models. Hosted provider calls followed the providers' terms of
service, and the external MCP Inspector instantiation used fixed synthetic
credentials. No experiment accessed a real account, filesystem resource, or
protected network service.

The Inspector study establishes a documented storage configuration, not a
third-party vulnerability, and its official scope gates behaved as specified;
no coordinated disclosure was required. Released stimuli contain prompt
injections against a fictitious tool schema. They cannot address an
operational service, while their release is necessary to reproduce the
reported finite-bank measurements.
