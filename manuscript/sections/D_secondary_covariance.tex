\section{Secondary Semantic-Placement Fixture}
\label{app:secondary-covariance}

An earlier semantic-placement fixture measured whether the response factor is
portable across endpoints and operationalizations. The DeepSeek confirmation
(freeze \texttt{lifecycle-covariance-deepseek-20260828-v1}) produced
elevated-state write attempts on 42/48 payloads (0.875, exact 95\% CI
$[0.748,0.953]$; every family nonzero, 6/8--8/8) and no untrusted-state
attempts. Its four-state table of (attempts, mutations) was $(0/48,0/48)$ at
$(0,0)$ and $(0,1)$, $(42/48,0/48)$ at $(1,0)$, and $(42/48,42/48)$ at
$(1,1)$. The Qwen replication produced elevated-state writes on 47/48
(CI $[0.8893,0.9995]$) and untrusted-state writes on 28/48. Both confirmations
passed all 24/24 authorized topology replays.

Qwen's sensitivity was $19/48=0.395833$ (19 payloads moved from no attempt to
attempt under elevation and none reversed; exact McNemar
$p=3.8\times10^{-6}$). The declared covariances predict excess risks of
$0.023141\times0.395833=0.009160$ and
$0.047500\times0.395833=0.018802$, matching the weighted outcomes to six
decimals. DeepSeek provides the $\rzero=0$ limiting case with
$\rone=42/48$, for which the shared grant realizes the $1/q=20$ ratio at
$q=0.05$. Qwen yields 1.62 because it already attempts the write without
elevation. The paired cross-provider difference in the untrusted state was 28
Qwen-only versus 0 DeepSeek-only payloads (exact McNemar
$p=7.5\times10^{-9}$).

Qwen's nonzero $\rzero$ recurred across three banks: 28/48 in the lifecycle
bank, 29/48 for bare directives, and 32/48 for neutral carriers. Its
sensitivity was 0.3958 in this semantic-placement fixture and 0.3125 in the
credential-gated fixture, an absolute difference of 0.0833. The older
cross-endpoint contrast was 0.8750 versus 0.3958. One repeated endpoint across
two operationalizations cannot separate provider variance from fixture
variance or identify sensitivity as a provider-only property.
